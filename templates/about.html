About CNG Buddy

CNG Buddy is a smart fuel management platform designed to reduce waiting time at CNG stations and make refueling faster, easier, and smarter.

Why CNG Buddy?
  • Long queues waste time and fuel
  • No visibility of pump crowd status
  • Unplanned refueling causes delays
Our Solution

CNG Buddy provides live queue updates, nearest pump discovery, and advance booking for stress-free refueling.

Key Features

Nearest Pump Locator

Find nearby CNG pumps instantly

Live Queue Status

Know waiting time before reaching

Slot Booking

Book refueling slots in advance

Secure Payments

Fast and safe online payments

Admin Analytics

Monitor queues, revenue & usage

Our Mission

Our mission is to make CNG refueling stress-free, efficient, and time-saving for millions of CNG vehicle users across India.
